This takes about two minutes.

## 1. Open Zapier

Sign in to [zapier.com](https://zapier.com) and click **Create Zap** (or open any existing Zap you want to add a WundertreOS step to).

## 2. Search for WundertreOS

In the trigger or action picker, search for **WundertreOS**. Click the app card.

## 3. Connect a new account

When you pick any WundertreOS trigger or action, Zapier prompts you to **Connect a new account**. Click it.

## 4. Approve the OAuth consent screen

A WundertreOS window pops up. If you're not signed in, sign in to your WundertreOS workspace.

You'll see a consent screen listing the permissions Zapier is requesting:

- **Read your contacts, deals, and activities** (`contacts:read`)
- **Create, update, and delete contacts** (`contacts:write`)
- **Manage webhook subscriptions** (`webhooks:write`)

Pick the workspace you want to connect (if you belong to several) and click **Authorize**.

## 5. Done

Zapier confirms the connection with a green checkmark — usually showing the user and workspace name from [`GET /oauth-me`](/oauth/me) (e.g. *"Jane Doe @ Acme"*).

You can now pick a trigger or action and continue building your Zap.

<Callout type="tip">
You can connect <strong>multiple</strong> WundertreOS accounts to Zapier (e.g. one per workspace). Each Zap step lets you pick which connected account to use.
</Callout>

## Disconnecting

To revoke Zapier's access:

- In Zapier: **My Apps → WundertreOS → Disconnect**, or
- In WundertreOS: **Settings → Connected apps → Zapier → Revoke**

Either action calls [`POST /oauth-revoke`](/oauth/revoke) and immediately invalidates the tokens.
